Intrinsic value is the real worth of a company beyond its current market price.Traditional valuation methods mainly rely on assumptions and financial data alone.Important qualitative factors such as market sentiment and management quality are often ignored.Existing systems usually analyze financials, sentiment, and growth separately instead of combining them into one platform.Our project proposes an AI-driven integrated system that combines financial analysis, sentiment analysis, management evaluation, and growth prediction to estimate intrinsic value more effectively.

To build a data-driven platform that integrates financial fundamentals, ML-based growth
estimation, sentiment analysis, and AI-based management scoring to compute intrinsic
value using the Discounted Cash Flow model.
